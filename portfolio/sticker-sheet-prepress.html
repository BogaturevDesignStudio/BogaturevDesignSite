About

This project involved preparing an A3 sticker sheet according to the supplier's production requirements. The original sticker dimensions provided in the PSD files were preserved without scaling. The stickers were arranged on a 297 × 420 mm A3 sheet to use the available area as efficiently as possible while maintaining the required production spacing. A 2 mm white contour was created around each sticker, followed by a kiss-cut line and an additional die-cut line positioned 1 mm outside the kiss-cut. All cutting lines were separated into dedicated layers and the final artwork was prepared as an editable production file.
