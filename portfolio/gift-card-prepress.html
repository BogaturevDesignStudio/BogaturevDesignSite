About

This project involved preparing a gift card artwork for print production. The supplied design was adapted into a production-ready file with the required bleed area and trim setup. The artwork was prepared according to the printing specifications to ensure that all graphic elements extended correctly beyond the cutting line and that the final printed card could be trimmed accurately. The prepared files were approved and used for the production of the final gift cards.
