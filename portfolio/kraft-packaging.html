About

This project involved developing a packaging design according to specific printing requirements for kraft cardboard. The artwork was created in black and white for production using black ink, taking into account the characteristics and limitations of printing on kraft material. The design elements were developed specifically for the packaging structure and prepared for further production. The final artwork was adjusted to the required dimensions and prepared as a production-ready file according to the printing specifications.
