About

This project involved the development of a step-by-step assembly instruction for a metal-frame tent structure.

Using photographs of real construction components provided by the client, I created clear technical illustrations showing individual parts, connection points and the assembly sequence.

The goal was to transform photographic references into a consistent visual instruction that makes the assembly process easier to understand.
